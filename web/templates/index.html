WorkflowMVP turns prose or a short recording into structured actions, then Gemini emits Python against a curated tool registry. Parsing is rule-first, Gemini can repair ambiguous steps, and generated scripts run locally under CPU/memory rlimits — a constrained demo, not an OS sandbox.
